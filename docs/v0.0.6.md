# Moni v0.0.6

Moni Shot now covers what Shottr does — scrolling capture, OCR, 15 editing tools — with a cleaner, Apple-like look.

**Capture** (<kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>, then)
- Pixel loupe while selecting.
- <kbd>Space</kbd> window capture · <kbd>Enter</kbd> last area again · <kbd>3</kbd>/<kbd>5</kbd> timer.
- <kbd>S</kbd> **scrolling capture**: drag over the part that scrolls; Moni scrolls it with a virtual wheel and stitches one tall image. Sticky headers and footers appear once. Stitching runs in a separate helper process.
- <kbd>T</kbd> **copy text** (OCR) from an area.

**Editor**
- Redesigned: icon toolbar in the header, style bar at the bottom, light and dark mode.
- New tools: line, oval, blur, smart erase, spotlight, crop — plus the pen and move tool from #1.
- Select to move, resize by handles, delete or nudge; color, width and fill restyle the selection.
- <kbd>Shift</kbd> for 45° lines and squares, text bubbles and double-click editing, redo, zoom and pan.
- Gradient backdrop with rounded corners and shadow.
- OCR button and a QR bar with *Open* / *Copy*.

**OCR and QR** are off by default. Settings → Screenshot & recording → *Text recognition* installs `tesseract-ocr` (English + Korean) and `zbar-tools` through apt behind the desktop password prompt. New key: `ocr`.

Closes #1 (freehand pen, moving annotations).

Validated on GNOME 46 in an isolated headless shell: editor rendering for all tools, move/resize/undo/redo, crop and backdrop export (`make test-ui`), synthetic stitch test (`make test`), and real area / window / last-area / timer / scrolling captures (a 120-line window stitched to 2995 px with no gaps or repeats). OCR and QR recognition, scrolling capture in browsers, and the GNOME 42–44 runtime were not tested.
